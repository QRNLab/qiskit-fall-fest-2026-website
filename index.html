What is it?

A community celebration of quantum

Qiskit Fall Fest is a collection of quantum computing events organized by local communities around the globe during a single season. Our chapter brings together students, researchers, developers, and the curious to learn, build, and celebrate quantum technology together. Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 is a global series of student- and community-led quantum computing events taking place from October through November 2026. This year's theme celebrates a decade of quantum on the cloud, marking the 10th anniversary of IBM's first open-access quantum computer.

Proudly hosted at QRNLab, University of Chittagong. We are the only group from Bangladesh exclusively selected this year by IBM Quantum to bring this global celebration to our local community.

Questions

Frequently asked

Quick answers to the things people ask us most.

Do I need prior quantum experience?

No. Most workshops assume no background in quantum computing. A basic familiarity with Python is helpful but not required.

Is the event free?

Yes, all Fall Fest events are free to attend. You only need to register so we can keep you updated.

Can I attend remotely?

Workshops and talks will be hybrid where possible, and the hackathon will have an online track.

Do I need my own computer?

A laptop is recommended for workshops and the hackathon. Cloud-based notebooks will be provided for exercises.
