What is it?

A community celebration of quantum

Qiskit Fall Fest is a collection of quantum computing events organized by local communities around the globe during a single season. Our chapter brings together students, researchers, developers, and the curious to learn, build, and celebrate quantum technology together.

Questions

Frequently asked

Quick answers to the things people ask us most.

Do I need prior quantum experience?

No. Most workshops assume no background in quantum computing. A basic familiarity with Python is helpful but not required.

Is the event free?

Yes, all Fall Fest events are free to attend. You only need to register so we can keep you updated.

Can I attend remotely?

Workshops and talks will be hybrid where possible, and the hackathon will have an online track.

Do I need my own computer?

A laptop is recommended for workshops and the hackathon. Cloud-based notebooks will be provided for exercises.
